What this does not tell you

  • Impermanent loss is measured at the end price only. It ignores the path price took to get there.
  • Fees are an assumption you provide. Real fees depend on trading volume and how much liquidity is in your range.
  • v3 fees are scaled from a full-range yield, which tends to overstate them. Treat v3 results as an upper bound.
  • Positions are static: no rebalancing, no gas costs. This is a scenario tool, not a forecast or financial advice.
